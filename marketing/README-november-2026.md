## Editorial workflow
1. **Validate game context** (season, map, site, operator availability, and current game mechanics) against current official notes when a script contains specific patch-sensitive advice. Calendar scripts are intentionally evergreen; technical moves still require a test.
2. **Make the asset:** record authorized original gameplay in a private custom session or use owned footage. Put a single large hook on the first frame, readable captions, original audio, and one decision-based tip. Avoid copyrighted music and deceptive synthetic "live" game footage.
3. **Internal QA:** factual claims, player safety, voice clarity, landscape-to-portrait crop, visible screen text, platform duration, branding, licensed music, and coherent CTA.
4. **Publish only on Recon-owned connected platforms.** Inspect scheduled posts first to prevent duplicates. Log post ID, timestamp, video asset version, CTA, and UTM.
5. **Measure after publication:** watch time/completion, views, shares, saves where accessible, profile visits, free strat clicks, trials, coaching inquiry and attributed paid sessions. No evidence of conversion ≠ zero conversions.
6. **Improve:** revise the next script's first two seconds and single actionable takeaway based on observed retention. Never chase "millions of views" at the expense of player trust or sales quality.
