Goyo R6 Guide — Loadout, Sites, & How to Play in 2026

In Rainbow Six Siege, Goyo is a defender built around the Volcán Shield. Place Volcán shields on common vertical drop spots — when Sledge / Buck breaks the floor, you detonate the Volcán to deny the drop. Recon 6's plans pick Goyo at 9 sites, led by Cigar Room on Calypso Casino.

Goyo is the fire-denial defender. His Volcán Shields combine deployable shield protection with explosive fire denial. Strong on sites with vertical play (fire from Volcán denies floor breakers).

Where is Goyo strongest?

Goyo is strongest at Cigar Room on Calypso Casino, where Goyo is a flex defense pick. Goyo is picked at 9 sites: 9 flex. The plans pair Goyo most often with Mute (7 sites), Jager (5) and Mira (5).

Goyo's loadout and gadget

  • Role: Area Denial.
  • Gadget: Volcán Shield.
  • Primary: TCSG12 / Vector .45 ACP.
  • Secondary: P229.
  • Secondary gadget: Nitro Cell / Proximity Alarm.
  • Speed / armor: 2 / 2.

Two Volcán Shields with explosive canisters attached. Defenders can detonate the canister to create a fire wall in a wide area.

Ubisoft's Goyo page has the current loadout; SiegeGG's Goyo stats track pro picks.

Goyo's strengths

  • Volcán shields provide cover + fire denial.
  • Two shields = two area-denial zones.
  • TCSG12 is a strong shotgun.
  • Nitro Cell secondary.

Who counters Goyo?

Operators that counter Goyo:

  • Capitao (fire bolts can clear Volcán fire).
  • Twitch (drones detonate Volcán safely).

Playing against Goyo

On attack: shoot the Volcán canister from outside cover to detonate it — Goyo wastes the shield. Capitao fire bolts can clear deployed Volcán fire. Twitch drone darts detonate Volcáns from a distance.

Climbing ranked with Goyo

Place Volcán shields on common vertical drop spots — when Sledge / Buck breaks the floor, you detonate the Volcán to deny the drop. Position one shield in the bomb room for plant denial. Save 1 detonation for the post-plant.
