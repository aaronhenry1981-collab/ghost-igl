Mira R6 Guide — Loadout, Sites, & How to Play in 2026

In Rainbow Six Siege, Mira is a defender built around the Black Mirror. Place Mira windows on non-obvious walls — between rotation rooms, on the wall opposite the obvious bomb-pair wall. Recon 6's plans pick Mira at 24 sites (24 essential), led by Lockers on Bank.

Mira is the strongest intel anchor in the game. Her Black Mirror windows give defenders bulletproof intel through reinforced walls. Banned in 70%+ of Plat+ matches because her information advantage is round-deciding.

Mira's loadout and gadget

  • Role: Intel / Anchor.
  • Gadget: Black Mirror.
  • Primary: VECTOR .45 ACP / ITA12L.
  • Secondary: USP40.
  • Secondary gadget: Nitro Cell / Proximity Alarm.
  • Speed / armor: 2 / 2.

Two Black Mirror gadgets that create one-way bulletproof windows on reinforced walls. Defenders see through; attackers see only mirror reflection.

Ubisoft's Mira page has the current loadout; SiegeGG's Mira stats track pro picks.

Mira's strengths

  • Bulletproof one-way intel through reinforced walls.
  • Two Black Mirrors = two intel windows per round.
  • Strong primary (VECTOR .45 ACP).
  • Nitro Cell secondary for area denial.

Who counters Mira?

Operators that counter Mira:

  • Twitch (drones shoot Mira from outside).
  • Flores (RCE drones detonate near Mira).
  • Maverick (blowtorch through Mira reinforcement near canister).

Playing against Mira

On attack: Twitch drone darts can shoot the canister at the bottom of a Mira window through soft surfaces — destroys the Mira. Flores RCE drones detonate near Mira to clear them. Maverick can blowtorch the wall near a Mira window to expose her.

Climbing ranked with Mira

Place Mira windows on non-obvious walls — between rotation rooms, on the wall opposite the obvious bomb-pair wall. The non-standard placement reads attacker pushes 4-5 seconds before they realize what's happening. Drop the canister late — wait until attackers commit utility, then drop the canister to surprise them.
