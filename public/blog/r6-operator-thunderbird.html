Thunderbird R6 Guide — Loadout, Sites, & How to Play in 2026

In Rainbow Six Siege, Thunderbird is a defender built around the Kona Station. Three Kona Stations that heal anyone (defender OR attacker) who interacts with them. Place Kona Stations in central defender positions — anchors can heal between fights. Recon 6's plans pick Thunderbird at 17 sites, led by Kitchen on Villa.

Thunderbird is sustained heal. Her Kona Stations restore HP throughout the round, making her a strong anchor on sites with heavy frag damage. Banned in 20%+ of late-round metas.

Thunderbird's strengths

  • Three Kona Stations = three heal zones.
  • Strong primary (SPEAR .308).
  • Heals work passively (no active reset like Doc).
  • Versatile secondary.

Who counters Thunderbird?

Operators that counter Thunderbird:

  • Caveira (interrogate denies Thunderbird heals).
  • Frost (mats keep team in DBNO).

Playing against Thunderbird

On attack: identify Kona Station positions early — destroying them denies the heals. Caveira interrogate kills DBNO'd defenders before Kona heals trigger.

Climbing ranked with Thunderbird

Place Kona Stations in central defender positions — anchors can heal between fights. Position one in the bomb room for plant denial heals. Save 1 station for late-round repositioning if site collapses.
