Maestro R6 Guide — Loadout, Sites, & How to Play in 2026

In Rainbow Six Siege, Maestro is a defender built around the Evil Eye. Place Evil Eyes on commonly-planted bomb spots — defenders shoot the laser at the planter through the camera. Recon 6's plans pick Maestro at 27 sites (24 essential), led by Open Area on Bank.

Maestro is intel-and-damage in one. His Evil Eyes provide bulletproof cameras that deal damage to anyone Maestro shoots through them. Strong on sites with predictable plant spots — Maestro punishes the planter.

Maestro's strengths

  • Bulletproof cameras — can't be shot through normally.
  • Lasers damage attackers (chips them down for plant denial).
  • Two Evil Eyes per round.
  • Strong primary LMG (ALDA 5.56).

Who counters Maestro?

Operators that counter Maestro:

  • Twitch (drones can shoot Maestro lasers off when active).
  • Flores (RCE drones detonate Maestros).
  • Glaz (thermal scope sees Maestro target zones).

Playing against Maestro

On attack: Twitch drone darts disable Maestro lasers when they're active. Flores drones detonate Maestros via explosion. Maverick can blowtorch the wall near a Maestro to expose it.

Climbing ranked with Maestro

Place Evil Eyes on commonly-planted bomb spots — defenders shoot the laser at the planter through the camera. Position one Evil Eye on the wall where Maestro himself can shoot through it for double-damage. Save laser shots for the post-plant defuse window.
