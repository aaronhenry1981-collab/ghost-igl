Defense Utility Gate / Anti-Breach 2-speed / 2-armor

Aruni is the laser-gate defender. Her Surya gates create damage barriers on doorways and windows, denying entry through specific paths. Plus the gates destroy projectiles (grenades, breach charges) thrown through.

What is Aruni's loadout?

Aruni kit at a glance

  • Side: Defense
  • Role: Utility Gate / Anti-Breach
  • Gadget: Surya Gate
  • Primary: P10 RONI / Mk 14 EBR
  • Secondary: PRB92
  • Secondary gadget: Bulletproof Camera / Barbed Wire
  • Speed / armor: 2-speed / 2-armor

Three laser gates that activate on movement detection — damage attackers and destroy thrown projectiles. Doors block reinforced wall paths.

Strengths

  • Three Surya gates = three entry denials
  • Gates destroy thrown projectiles
  • Strong primary (P10 RONI)
  • Versatile secondary

Who counters Aruni?

Attacking into Aruni, these operators work directly against the kit:

  • Maverick (blowtorch through Surya-protected walls)
  • Twitch (drones disable gates)

How do you play against Aruni?

On attack: Maverick can blowtorch through walls Aruni Surya gates protect. Twitch drone darts disable gates. Throw grenades into gates — they're destroyed but the gate goes on cooldown.

How do you climb with Aruni?

Place Surya gates on commonly-used attacker entry routes — windows, doorways. The gate forces attackers to either break the gate (loud + slow) or take an alternate entry. Coordinate with Mira windows for combined intel + denial.
