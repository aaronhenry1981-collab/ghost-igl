Capitao R6 Guide — Loadout, Sites, & How to Play in 2026

In Rainbow Six Siege, Capitao is an attacker built around the Tactical Crossbow. Use fire bolts to force anchors off their default positions during exec. Recon 6's plans pick Capitao at 21 sites (2 essential), led by Open Area on Bank.

Capitao denies anchor positions with fire bolts and creates instant smoke cover with smoke bolts. Strong on sites with predictable anchor spots where the fire bolt forces defenders out of position.

Capitao's loadout and gadget

  • Role: Area Denial.
  • Gadget: Tactical Crossbow.
  • Primary: PARA-308 / M249.
  • Secondary: PRB92.
  • Secondary gadget: Frag Grenade / Claymore.
  • Speed / armor: 2 / 2.

A crossbow with two ammo types: smoke bolts (creates instant smoke for 6 seconds) and asphyxiation bolts (fire damage in a wide area).

Ubisoft's Capitao page has the current loadout; SiegeGG's Capitao stats track pro picks.

Capitao's strengths

  • Fire bolts create area denial that doesn't expire (until destroyed).
  • Smoke bolts give instant cover (no 4-second wait like Smoke grenade).
  • PARA-308 is a strong AR with high damage.
  • Versatile across attack scenarios.

Who counters Capitao?

Operators that counter Capitao:

  • Smoke (his canisters can clear Capitao fire bolts).
  • Goyo (Volcan shields counter Capitao fire denial).

Playing against Capitao

On defense: most teams don't bother countering Capitao directly — instead they reposition off the anchor he targets. Smoke can clear his fire bolts, but it's a niche play.

Climbing ranked with Capitao

Use fire bolts to force anchors off their default positions during exec. Save 1 fire bolt for late-round denial of post-plant defuse. Smoke bolts are excellent for instant cover during a coordinated rush — better than Smoke grenades because no fuse delay.
