Ying R6 Guide — Loadout, Sites, & How to Play in 2026

In Rainbow Six Siege, Ying is an attacker built around the Candela. Roll Candelas through doorways before pushing — the 4-flash chain disorients defenders for 6+ seconds. Recon 6's plans pick Ying at 16 sites, led by Consul Office on Consulate.

Ying is the master of disorientation. Her Candela cluster flashes any defender she rolls them at, and the passive lets her flash through soft floors. Strong on coordinated execs where flash timing wins the duel.

Ying's loadout and gadget

  • Role: Soft Breach / Flash.
  • Gadget: Candela.
  • Primary: T-95 LSW / SIX12.
  • Secondary: Q-929.
  • Secondary gadget: Smoke Grenade / Stun Grenade.
  • Speed / armor: 2 / 2.

Three Candela cluster grenades that flash defenders 4 times in succession. Each Candela = 4 individual flashes. Plus passive that lets her flash through soft floors and walls.

Ubisoft's Ying page has the current loadout; SiegeGG's Ying stats track pro picks.

Ying's strengths

  • Candela flashes 4 times in succession — defenders can't fully recover between.
  • Three Candela charges = 12 individual flashes per round.
  • Flashes work through soft floors (vertical play).
  • Strong primary LMG (T-95 LSW).

Who counters Ying?

Operators that counter Ying:

  • Echo (Yokai disorients Ying mid-flash).
  • Mute (jammers block Candela rolling activation).

Playing against Ying

On defense: when you hear Candela rolling sound, look at the floor or turn around. Echo Yokai disorients can interrupt Ying's flash play. Stay behind cover during exec — Candela flashes don't penetrate solid walls.

Climbing ranked with Ying

Roll Candelas through doorways before pushing — the 4-flash chain disorients defenders for 6+ seconds. Use the soft-floor passive on vertical execs (Bank CEO, Clubhouse Cash) — flash defenders below before dropping. Save 1 Candela for post-plant defuse cover.
