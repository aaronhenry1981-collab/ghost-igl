Castle R6 Guide — Loadout, Sites, & How to Play in 2026

In Rainbow Six Siege, Castle is a defender built around the Armor Panel. Four Castle armor barricades that block doors and windows with reinforced metal. Place Castle barricades on common attacker entry routes — windows, doorways. Recon 6's plans pick Castle at 5 sites, led by Lockers on Bank.

Castle denies attacker entry routes via reinforced barricades. Strong on sites with multiple choke points where forcing attackers into one entry is round-deciding.

Where is Castle strongest?

Castle is strongest at Lockers on Bank, where Castle is a flex defense pick. Castle is picked at 5 sites: 5 flex. The plans pair Castle most often with Smoke (5 sites), Mira (4) and Mozzie (3).

  • Lockers / CCTV Room on Bank (B): defense, flex, delay.
  • Customs Inspection / Supply Room on Border (1F): defense, flex, delay.
  • Wine Cellar / Snowmobile Garage on Chalet (B): defense, flex, delay.
  • Work Office / Exhibition on Skyscraper (2F): defense, flex, delay.
  • Kitchen / Dining Room on Villa (1F): defense, flex, delay.

Castle's strengths

  • Four armor barricades = four entry denials.
  • Forces attackers to bring soft-breach utility (Buck, Sledge, Zofia).
  • Strong primary SMG (UMP45).
  • Versatile secondary gadgets.

Who counters Castle?

Operators that counter Castle:

  • Sledge (hammer breaks barricades).
  • Buck (Skeleton Key shotgun).
  • Zofia (impact grenades).
  • Maverick (blowtorch).

Playing against Castle

On attack: bring soft-breach (Sledge, Buck, Zofia) for any Castle-banned site. As of Y11S3.1 an Armor Panel takes 10 melee hits to destroy (was 9).

Climbing ranked with Castle

Place Castle barricades on common attacker entry routes — windows, doorways. Force attackers to bring soft breach, removing one of their meta picks. Coordinate with Mira windows on adjacent walls — Castle denies one entry; Mira reads the other.
