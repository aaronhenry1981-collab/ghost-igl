Attack Hard Breach 3-speed / 1-armor

Hibana is the speedier alternative to Thermite. Her X-KAIROS pellets pierce reinforced walls without needing flush placement, and the 4-second timer (vs Thermite's 5) gives slightly faster execs. Smaller hole means tighter executions but better for vertical breach setups.

What is Hibana's loadout?

Hibana kit at a glance

  • Side: Attack
  • Role: Hard Breach
  • Gadget: X-KAIROS Pellet Launcher
  • Primary: TYPE-89 / SUPERNOVA
  • Secondary: BEARING 9 / P229
  • Secondary gadget: Smoke Grenade / Stun Grenade
  • Speed / armor: 3-speed / 1-armor

Six X-KAIROS pellets in three 2-pellet shots that pierce reinforced walls and create small holes. Activates after a 4-second timer.

Strengths

  • 3-speed mobility for fast peeks during exec
  • 4-second activation (faster than Thermite)
  • Pellets pierce walls without flush placement — works on awkward surfaces
  • Smoke / stun secondary enables varied utility

Who counters Hibana?

Defending against Hibana, these operators work directly against the kit:

  • Bandit (batteries trick pellets)
  • Kaid (wider Electroclaw range)
  • Mute (jammers block pellets)

How do you play against Hibana?

On defense: Bandit-trick on the count of Hibana's pellet placement sound. Pellets activate ~4 seconds after attaching, so battery placement window is tight but doable. Mute jammers block pellets entirely if the pellets land within jammer range.

How do you climb with Hibana?

Use Hibana for vertical breach (floors and hatches) — her pellets work on more surface types than Thermite. Run with Thatcher for the EMP combo. On verticals, place pellets from above on a soft hatch reinforcement to drop on defenders below. Smoke secondary enables post-plant lineups; choose stun if your team needs flash util instead.
