Bandit R6 Guide — Loadout, Sites, & How to Play in 2026

Bandit's loadout and gadget

  • Role: Anti-Breach.
  • Gadget: CED-1 Shock Wire.
  • Primary: MP7 / M870.
  • Secondary: B-9 / P12.
  • Secondary gadget: Nitro Cell / Barbed Wire.
  • Speed / armor: 3 / 1.

Four batteries that electrify any reinforced wall they're placed on. Disrupts Thermite/Hibana/Ace charges. Three-second active time before depletion.

Ubisoft's Bandit page has the current loadout; SiegeGG's Bandit stats track pro picks.

Bandit's strengths

  • Four batteries per round = four wall protections.
  • 3-speed mobility for tricking + roaming.
  • MP7 is a strong SMG.
  • Nitro Cell secondary for area denial.

Who counters Bandit?

Operators that counter Bandit:

  • Thatcher (EMP clears all Bandit batteries).
  • Twitch (drone darts destroy batteries).
  • Maverick (silent blowtorch bypasses batteries).

Playing against Bandit

On attack: bring Thatcher always. EMP at 1:50 timer clears all Bandit batteries on a wall. Twitch drone is the alt-counter — darts destroy batteries on walls Thatcher can't reach.

Climbing ranked with Bandit

Master the Bandit trick: place battery on the wall RIGHT before Hibana/Thermite/Ace places their charge. The trick window is 1-2 seconds — too early and the battery depletes; too late and the charge has already detonated. Practice in Custom Game.
