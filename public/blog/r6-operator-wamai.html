Wamai R6 Guide — Loadout, Sites, & How to Play in 2026

In Rainbow Six Siege, Wamai is a defender built around the Mag-NET System. Place Mag-NETs near choke points where attackers throw flashes/smokes. Recon 6's plans pick Wamai at 20 sites (1 essential), led by Hookah Lounge on Coastline.

Wamai is the next-gen Jager. His Mag-NETs pull projectiles toward themselves and detonate them away from defenders. Strong on sites where attackers rely on flash/grenade utility.

Wamai's loadout and gadget

  • Role: Utility Denial.
  • Gadget: Mag-NET System.
  • Primary: AUG A2 / MP5K.
  • Secondary: KERATOS .357 / P12.
  • Secondary gadget: Deployable Shield / Proximity Alarm.
  • Speed / armor: 2 / 2.

Five Mag-NET devices that magnetically pull thrown projectiles (grenades, breach charges) and detonate them safely. Strong against utility-heavy attack comps.

Ubisoft's Wamai page has the current loadout; SiegeGG's Wamai stats track pro picks.

Wamai's strengths

  • Pulls projectiles away from defender positions.
  • Five Mag-NETs = wider coverage than Jager ADS.
  • Strong primary AR (AUG A2).
  • Versatile secondary.

Who counters Wamai?

Operators that counter Wamai:

  • Twitch (drones destroy Mag-NETs).
  • IQ (scanner finds Mag-NETs).

Playing against Wamai

On attack: same as Jager — bring Twitch / IQ to clear. Throw multiple grenades simultaneously — Mag-NETs pull only one at a time.

Climbing ranked with Wamai

Place Mag-NETs near choke points where attackers throw flashes/smokes. Position one near the bomb default plant for late-round nitro pulls. Coordinate with Jager for combined utility denial.
