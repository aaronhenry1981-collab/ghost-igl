Zofia R6 Guide — Loadout, Sites, & How to Play in 2026

In Rainbow Six Siege, Zofia is an attacker built around the KS79 LIFELINE. Use concussion grenades to disorient anchors during exec, then push through the disorientation window. Recon 6's plans pick Zofia at 38 sites (5 essential), led by Kitchen on Chalet.

Zofia is a versatile entry attacker with soft breach + concussion utility plus a unique self-revive (Withstand). Her LMG-E gives her sustained fire for entry duels, and her impact grenades create rotation paths.

Zofia's loadout and gadget

  • Role: Soft Breach / Self-Revive Entry.
  • Gadget: KS79 LIFELINE.
  • Primary: LMG-E / M762.
  • Secondary: RG15.
  • Secondary gadget: Breach Charge / Claymore.
  • Speed / armor: 2 / 2.

A grenade launcher that fires impact grenades (soft breach) and concussion grenades (disorients defenders). Plus the Withstand passive (self-revive).

Ubisoft's Zofia page has the current loadout; SiegeGG's Zofia stats track pro picks.

Zofia's strengths

  • Self-revive from down state (Withstand passive — once per round).
  • Both soft breach AND concussion utility in one gadget.
  • LMG-E gives high mag size for entry duels.
  • Versatile across maps.

Who counters Zofia?

Operators that counter Zofia:

  • Caveira (DBNO interrogate prevents Withstand activation).
  • Frost (mats keep Zofia in DBNO before she can self-revive).

Playing against Zofia

On defense: Caveira interrogate denies Zofia's self-revive (the interrogate kills her in DBNO). Frost mats can also pin her down before she can self-revive. Don't rush a downed Zofia — let her bleed out or interrogate.

Climbing ranked with Zofia

Use concussion grenades to disorient anchors during exec, then push through the disorientation window. Save Withstand passive for clutch 1v1s — don't commit to it as guaranteed revive. Impact grenades create soft breach paths for rotations and post-plant cover.
