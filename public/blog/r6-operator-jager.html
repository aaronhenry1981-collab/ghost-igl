Jager R6 Guide — Loadout, Sites, & How to Play in 2026

In Rainbow Six Siege, Jager is a defender who plays as utility denial. Place ADS units near key choke points where attackers throw flashes/smokes for exec. Recon 6's plans pick Jager at 46 sites (4 essential), led by Gym on Clubhouse.

Jager's loadout and gadget

  • Role: Utility Denial.
  • Gadget: ADS-MK IV "Magpie".
  • Primary: 416-C CARBINE / M870.
  • Secondary: P12 / SMG-12.
  • Secondary gadget: Bulletproof Camera / Impact Grenade.
  • Speed / armor: 3 / 1.

Three ADS gadgets that intercept thrown projectiles (grenades, breach charges, smoke grenades) within their radius. Each ADS can intercept 2 projectiles before deactivating.

Ubisoft's Jager page has the current loadout; SiegeGG's Jager stats track pro picks.

Jager's strengths

  • Three ADS units = up to 6 projectile intercepts per round.
  • 3-speed mobility for repositioning.
  • Strong primary AR (416-C).
  • Versatile secondary gadgets.

Who counters Jager?

Operators that counter Jager:

  • Twitch (drones shoot ADS units).
  • IQ (scanner finds ADS through walls).
  • Thatcher (EMP disables ADS for 9 seconds).

Playing against Jager

On attack: Twitch drone darts destroy ADS units. Thatcher EMPs disable them temporarily. Throw multiple grenades simultaneously — ADS can only intercept 2 projectiles before depletion.

Climbing ranked with Jager

Place ADS units near key choke points where attackers throw flashes/smokes for exec. Position one near the bomb default plant for late-round nitro denial. Don't cluster ADS units — spread them across the site.
