Glaz R6 Guide — Loadout, Sites, & How to Play in 2026

In Rainbow Six Siege, Glaz is an attacker who plays as sniper / long-range pick. Throw your own smoke at site, then thermal-scope through it for free picks. Recon 6's plans pick Glaz at 4 sites, led by Consul Office on Consulate.

Glaz is the only true sniper on attack. His OTs-03 1-taps any defender on a headshot, and the thermal scope reads heat signatures through Glaz-thrown smoke for free picks. Strong on long-sightline maps (Coastline, Border, Plane).

Where is Glaz strongest?

Glaz is strongest at Consul Office on Consulate, where Glaz is a flex attack pick. Glaz is picked at 4 sites: 4 flex. The plans pair Glaz most often with Buck (4 sites), Kali (3) and Maverick (3).

  • Consul Office / Meeting Room on Consulate (2F): attack, flex, flex / intel.
  • Garage / Cafeteria on Consulate (B): attack, flex, flex / intel.
  • Bar / Lounge on Emerald Plains (1F): attack, flex, flex / intel.
  • Laundry / Piano on Outback (2F): attack, flex, flex / intel.

Glaz's strengths

  • 1-shot headshot kill at any range.
  • Thermal scope sees through smoke (Glaz's own smoke grenades).
  • Long-range sightline control.
  • Smoke secondary creates the smoke he can see through.

Who counters Glaz?

Operators that counter Glaz:

  • Smoke (his canisters block thermal scope).
  • Castle (barricades deny Glaz long sightlines).

Playing against Glaz

On defense: don't hold long sightlines against Glaz unless you have hard cover. His thermal scope means smokes don't protect you. Castle barricades on common Glaz angles deny his sightlines entirely.

Climbing ranked with Glaz

Throw your own smoke at site, then thermal-scope through it for free picks. Use long-sightline maps where Glaz can hold an angle from spawn through the site. Save smoke for the post-plant, not for entry — Glaz at long range with thermal scope is your strength.
