What Operation Split Fire brought

From Ubisoft's official Operation Split Fire season page:

  • Noor — a new Defender built to counter shield operators; his Horus Lance Launcher fires projectiles that pierce shields and destructible surfaces before erupting into flames.
  • Legend Division — a SoloQ-only Ranked playlist for eligible top players, which Ubisoft says opens mid-season.
  • Villa targeted map update — the basement and garage were redesigned, and the Living Room / Library bomb site moved downstairs.
  • Balance changes — to operators and weapons at launch, followed by the Y11S3.1 patch on September 22, 2026.

How to spend the time left this season

Season resets compress your MMR toward the middle — the players who climb fastest after a reset are the ones who fixed their fundamentals before it. Two honest suggestions:

  • Learn the ranked pool properly: site-by-site strats for every ranked map — free tier covers the whole pool.
  • Find out what actually costs you rounds: drop a screenshot for an AI breakdown, or browse the operator index and live meta board.
