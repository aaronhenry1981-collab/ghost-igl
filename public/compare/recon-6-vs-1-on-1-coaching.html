Recon 6 vs a 1-on-1 coaching session

By Aaron Henry, founder of Recon 6 · Published October 6, 2026

Drafted with AI assistance from Recon 6's strat library and notes, then published by Aaron Henry. Check gadget and season details against the official sources at the end. If something doesn't match your game, tell us.

Recon 6 Pro is a $12-a-month Rainbow Six Siege coaching tool: a plan for the round you're about to play, 20 AI reviews of your own screenshots a month, and a Windows desktop coach. A 1-on-1 session is an hour with a human coach going through your rounds with you.

Which costs less?

Pro costs $12 a month. One session costs $40, and your first is $20. Two sessions a month would be $80; Champion is $70 a month and includes 2 sessions plus everything in Elite.

What does a human coach catch that software can't?

A coach sees the whole round: your timing, your comms, the sound cue you ignored, and the decision ten seconds before the death. Recon 6's review only sees the screenshots you upload, so it can only judge what is in them.

What does Recon 6 do that one session can't?

It is there every match. Pick the map, site and side and you get one plan with five operator jobs, the callouts and the utility. After the match, Pro reviews screenshots from your rounds and keeps the mistake you need to fix in front of you. Bank and Coastline are free to try.

Which should you pick?

If you don't know what you're doing wrong, start with one session; the first is $20. If you know the problem and need reps with a plan, Pro is the cheaper habit. If you want both every month, Champion covers them. Elite ($39) sits between: more reviews, no live sessions.

Questions

Is Recon 6 a replacement for a coach?
No. It covers the plan before the round and a review of your screenshots after it. A coach watches the whole round and talks it through with you, which software doesn't do.
Can I book a session without a subscription?
Yes. Single sessions are $40 an hour with no subscription, and your first is $20.
Who runs the sessions?
Aaron Henry, who founded Recon 6. Sessions are booked from the coaching page.
