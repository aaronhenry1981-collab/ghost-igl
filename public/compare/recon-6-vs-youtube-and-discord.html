What do YouTube guides do well?

They teach. A good guide shows why a setup works, how pros play it and what the angles look like. They're free and there are a lot of them. The catch is finding the right one for the exact site and side with prep running, and knowing whether it still matches the current season.

What do Discord strat sheets do well?

A sheet your stack keeps is built around how you play, and everyone agreed to it. It only stays useful while someone keeps it updated, and it rarely says what each of the five players should do.

What does Recon 6 add?

Speed and structure: one plan for the map, site and side you picked, with a job for each of the five players, the callouts and the utility. Plans cover 25 maps. With Pro, Recon 6 also reviews screenshots from your own rounds and points out the mistake to fix next. The strats are in beta, so if a plan doesn't match your game, tell us.

Should you stop watching guides?

No. Watch a guide to understand an idea, open Recon 6 to get the plan for the round, play it, then review it. They do different jobs.

Questions

Is Recon 6 free?
Bank and Coastline are free to open without paying. Pro is $12 a month for advanced strategies, AI review and the desktop coach.
Does Recon 6 replace my stack's Discord?
No. Recon 6 gives everyone the same plan to start from; your stack's own calls and adjustments still matter.
Are the strats current?
Plans follow Ubisoft's current map list, and a plan that can't be backed up is withdrawn instead of guessed. They are marked beta.
