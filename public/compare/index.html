Compare Recon 6 with other ways to improve

Recon 6 is one way to get better at Rainbow Six Siege. These pages put it next to the others: a human coach, free YouTube guides and Discord strat sheets, and the other kinds of coaching tools. Each comparison gives the cost, what each option does well, and its limits.

Which comparison do you need?

  • Recon 6 vs a 1-on-1 coaching session. Recon 6 Pro is a $12-a-month Rainbow Six Siege coaching tool: a plan for the round you're about to play, 20 AI reviews of your own screenshots a month, and a Windows desktop coach. A 1-on-1 session is an hour with a human coach going through your rounds with you.
  • Recon 6 vs YouTube guides and Discord strat sheets. YouTube guides and Discord strat sheets are the free ways most players learn Rainbow Six Siege. Recon 6 is a strat tool built around the round in front of you: pick the map, site and side, and get one plan with five operator jobs.
  • Rainbow Six Siege coaching tools, by type. A Rainbow Six Siege coaching tool is anything that helps you play better rounds: a strat reference for the plan, a stat tracker for your numbers, VOD review for your mistakes, live coaching, or structured practice. Each one fixes a different problem.

What does each option cost?

YouTube guides, Discord strat sheets and Recon 6's Bank and Coastline plans are free. Recon 6 Pro is $12 a month and Elite is $39. A 1-on-1 session is $40 an hour, and your first is $20. Champion is $70 a month and includes 2 sessions. Recon 6 has no free trial.
