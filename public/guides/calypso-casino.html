Calypso Casino — Complete Strategy Guide

In Rainbow Six Siege, Calypso Casino is a ranked map this season with 4 bomb sites: Cigar Room (2F), Blackjack (1F), Bar (1F) and CCTV (B). Recon 6's plans on Calypso Casino lean on Thatcher and Thermite for attack and on Kaid and Maestro for defense.

How should you play Calypso Casino's bomb sites?

Cigar Room / Pool (2F)

On Cigar Room, Thermite and Thatcher lead the attack; Kaid and Mute lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Thermite (hard breach), Thatcher (support). Recommended: Buck (vertical play), Iana (entry frag). Flex: Nomad (flank watch).

Hard breach the Pool wall with Thermite while Thatcher EMPs the anti-breach.

Callouts: Cigar Room, Pool, High Roller, Upper Balcony.

Defense

Essential: Kaid (anti-breach), Mute (intel denial). Recommended: Valkyrie (intel), Vigil (roam). Flex: Goyo (flex / denial).

Reinforce the Pool wall and the hatch above; Kaid electrifies against the hard breach.

Callouts: Cigar Room, Pool, High Roller, Upper Balcony.

Blackjack / Poker (1F)

On Blackjack, Ace and Thatcher lead the attack; Bandit and Maestro lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Ace (hard breach), Thatcher (support). Recommended: Zofia (soft breach), Ash (entry frag). Flex: Flores (utility clear).

Open the Poker wall with Ace; Thatcher clears anti-breach.

Callouts: Blackjack, Poker, Side Hall, Main Entrance.

Defense

Essential: Bandit (anti-breach), Maestro (intel / denial). Recommended: Mute (utility denial), Alibi (roam / intel). Flex: Melusi (slow / flex).

Bandit-trick or Kaid the Poker wall.

Callouts: Blackjack, Poker, Side Hall, Main Entrance.

Bar / Betting (1F)

On Bar, Thermite and Thatcher lead the attack; Kaid and Mira lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Thermite (hard breach), Thatcher (support). Recommended: Buck (vertical play), Ash (entry frag). Flex: Jackal (flank / intel).

Open the Betting Lounge wall with Thermite; Thatcher clears anti-breach.

Callouts: Bar, Main Entrance, Betting Lounge, Upper Balcony.

Defense

Essential: Kaid (anti-breach), Mira (intel). Recommended: Mute (utility denial), Ela (roam). Flex: Goyo (flex / denial).

Reinforce the Betting Lounge wall; Kaid electrifies.

Callouts: Bar, Main Entrance, Betting Lounge, Upper Balcony.

CCTV / Vault Checkpoint (B)

On CCTV, Hibana and Thatcher lead the attack; Mute and Maestro lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Hibana (hard breach), Thatcher (support). Recommended: Sledge (vertical play), Iana (entry frag). Flex: Nomad (flank watch).

Basement is a vertical and control fight.

Callouts: CCTV, Vault Checkpoint, Surveillance, Basement.

Defense

Essential: Mute (anti-breach / denial), Maestro (intel / denial). Recommended: Pulse (intel), Vigil (roam). Flex: Doc (sustain / anchor).

Basement defends on tight angles and hatches.

Callouts: CCTV, Vault Checkpoint, Surveillance, Basement.
