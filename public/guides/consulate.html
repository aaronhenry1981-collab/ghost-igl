Consulate — Complete Strategy Guide

In Rainbow Six Siege, Consulate is a ranked map this season with 4 bomb sites: Consul Office (2F), Piano Room (1F), Garage (B) and Tellers (1F). Recon 6's plans on Consulate lean on Kali and Maverick for attack and on Maestro and Bandit for defense.

How should you play Consulate's bomb sites?

Consul Office / Meeting Room (2F)

On Consul Office, Maverick and Kali lead the attack; Bandit and Maestro lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Maverick (hard breach), Kali (support). Recommended: Buck (vertical play), Ying (entry frag). Flex: Glaz (flex / intel).

Map control first: Glaz and Ying clear Consul Office side while Buck sets up vertical pressure.

Callouts: Meeting Room, Consul Office, Front Lobby, Back Stairs.

Defense

Essential: Bandit (anti-breach), Maestro (intel). Recommended: Wamai (utility denial), Alibi (roam). Flex: Smoke (flex / sustain).

Bandit denies the hard-breach on Meeting Room with electrified walls.

Callouts: Meeting Room, Consul Office, Front Lobby, Back Stairs.

Piano Room / Exposition Room (1F)

On Piano Room, Maverick and Kali lead the attack; Mute and Maestro lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Maverick (hard breach), Kali (support). Recommended: Zofia (vertical play), Ying (entry frag). Flex: Fuze (flex / intel).

Maverick opens the Piano Room reinforced wall while Kali clears anti-breach electronics.

Callouts: Piano Room, Exposition Room, Front Lobby, Back Stairs.

Defense

Essential: Mute (anti-breach), Maestro (intel). Recommended: Jager (utility denial), Vigil (roam). Flex: Mozzie (flex / sustain).

Hold Exposition Room as the trade lane — Mozzie anchors there.

Callouts: Piano Room, Exposition Room, Front Lobby, Back Stairs.

Garage / Cafeteria (B)

On Garage, Maverick and Kali lead the attack; Bandit and Maestro lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Maverick (hard breach), Kali (support). Recommended: Buck (vertical play), Ying (entry frag). Flex: Glaz (flex / intel).

Open with Kali clearing electronics, then Maverick hits the Cafeteria wall on call.

Callouts: Cafeteria, Garage, Front Lobby, Back Stairs.

Defense

Essential: Bandit (anti-breach), Maestro (intel). Recommended: Wamai (utility denial), Pulse (roam). Flex: Doc (flex / sustain).

Anchored 4, roaming 1: Doc, Wamai, Bandit, and Maestro hold site; Pulse plays off-site for pick potential and timing pressure.

Callouts: Cafeteria, Garage, Front Lobby, Back Stairs.

Tellers / Servers (1F)

On Tellers, Hibana and Thatcher lead the attack; Mute and Echo lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Hibana (hard breach), Thatcher (support). Recommended: Zofia (vertical play), Ash (entry frag). Flex: Fuze (flex / intel).

Zofia sets the tempo — Zofia plays vertical from 2F to pressure Tellers anchors through the soft floor.

Callouts: Tellers, Servers, Front Lobby, Back Stairs.

Defense

Essential: Mute (anti-breach), Echo (intel). Recommended: Jager (utility denial), Caveira (roam). Flex: Lesion (flex / sustain).

Setup is intel-first: Echo covers site entries with cameras and feeds rotation calls.

Callouts: Tellers, Servers, Front Lobby, Back Stairs.
