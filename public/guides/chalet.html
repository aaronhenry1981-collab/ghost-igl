Chalet — Complete Strategy Guide

In Rainbow Six Siege, Chalet is a ranked map this season with 4 bomb sites: Master Bedroom (2F), Bar (1F), Kitchen (1F) and Wine Cellar (B). Recon 6's plans on Chalet lean on Thatcher and Thermite for attack and on Smoke and Bandit for defense.

How should you play Chalet's bomb sites?

Master Bedroom / Office (2F)

On Master Bedroom, Hibana and Thatcher lead the attack; Kaid and Smoke lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Hibana (hard breach), Thatcher (support). Recommended: Ash (entry frag), Zofia (soft breach). Flex: Nomad (flank watch).

Hibana opens the Master Bedroom wall from the balcony at range.

Callouts: Master Bedroom, Office, Balcony, Main Stairs.

Defense

Essential: Kaid (anti-breach), Smoke (area denial). Recommended: Jager (utility denial), Vigil (roam). Flex: Melusi (slow).

Kaid electrifies the Master Bedroom wall from the bathroom side.

Callouts: Master Bedroom, Office, Balcony, Main Stairs.

Bar / Gaming Room (1F)

On Bar, Thermite and Thatcher lead the attack; Bandit and Maestro lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Thermite (hard breach), Thatcher (support). Recommended: Buck (vertical play), Capitao (area denial). Flex: Iana (intel).

Thermite opens the Bar reinforced wall from the campfire side.

Callouts: Bar, Gaming Room, Campfire, Main Entrance.

Defense

Essential: Bandit (anti-breach), Maestro (intel / denial). Recommended: Jager (utility denial), Ela (roam). Flex: Thunderbird (sustain).

Bandit tricks the Bar wall to deny Thermite.

Callouts: Bar, Gaming Room, Campfire, Main Entrance.

Kitchen / Dining Room (1F)

On Kitchen, Ace and Zofia lead the attack; Smoke and Mira lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Ace (hard breach), Zofia (soft breach). Recommended: Sledge (vertical play), Nomad (flank watch). Flex: Finka (support).

Ace opens the Kitchen reinforced wall from the campfire side.

Callouts: Kitchen, Dining Room, Campfire, Back Stairs.

Defense

Essential: Smoke (area denial), Mira (intel). Recommended: Jager (utility denial), Vigil (roam). Flex: Lesion (intel / delay).

Mira places a Black Mirror between Kitchen and Dining Room for intel on the campfire push.

Callouts: Kitchen, Dining Room, Campfire, Back Stairs.

Wine Cellar / Snowmobile Garage (B)

On Wine Cellar, Thermite and Thatcher lead the attack; Bandit and Smoke lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Thermite (hard breach), Thatcher (support). Recommended: Buck (vertical play), Gridlock (flank denial). Flex: Lion (intel).

Thermite opens the Snowmobile Garage wall from the exterior.

Callouts: Wine Cellar, Snowmobile Garage, Wine Cellar Stairs, Back Stairs.

Defense

Essential: Bandit (anti-breach), Smoke (area denial). Recommended: Mute (intel denial), Pulse (intel). Flex: Castle (delay).

Bandit tricks the Snowmobile Garage wall every round to deny Thermite.

Callouts: Wine Cellar, Snowmobile Garage, Wine Cellar Stairs, Back Stairs.
