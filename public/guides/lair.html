Lair — Complete Strategy Guide

In Rainbow Six Siege, Lair is a ranked map this season with 4 bomb sites: Master Office (2F), Bunks (1F), Armory (1F) and Lab (B). Recon 6's plans on Lair lean on Ace and Twitch for attack and on Mira and Mute for defense.

How should you play Lair's bomb sites?

Master Office / R6 Room (2F)

On Master Office, Ace and Twitch lead the attack; Mute and Mira lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Ace (hard breach), Twitch (support). Recommended: Zofia (vertical play), Iana (entry frag). Flex: Capitao (flex / intel).

Layered execute: Capitao flashes from main, Iana pushes R6 Room for the trade, Ace and Twitch hit Master Office simultaneously.

Callouts: Master Office, R6 Room, Sniper Nest, Garage.

Defense

Essential: Mute (anti-breach), Mira (intel). Recommended: Jager (utility denial), Alibi (roam). Flex: Azami (flex / sustain).

Alibi forces attackers to play slow — early picks from off-site keep their utility on flank-watch.

Callouts: Master Office, R6 Room, Sniper Nest, Garage.

Bunks / Briefing (1F)

On Bunks, Maverick and Kali lead the attack; Mute and Maestro lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Maverick (hard breach), Kali (support). Recommended: Zofia (vertical play), Ying (entry frag). Flex: Fuze (flex / intel).

Open with Kali clearing electronics, then Maverick hits the Bunks wall on call.

Callouts: Bunks, Briefing, Sniper Nest, Garage.

Defense

Essential: Mute (anti-breach), Maestro (intel). Recommended: Jager (utility denial), Caveira (roam). Flex: Thunderbird (flex / sustain).

Anchored 4, roaming 1: Thunderbird, Jager, Mute, and Maestro hold site; Caveira plays off-site for pick potential and timing pressure.

Callouts: Bunks, Briefing, Sniper Nest, Garage.

Armory / Weapon Maintenance (1F)

On Armory, Ace and Twitch lead the attack; Bandit and Mira lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Ace (hard breach), Twitch (support). Recommended: Buck (vertical play), Iana (entry frag). Flex: Nomad (flex / intel).

Layered execute: Nomad flashes from main, Iana pushes Weapon Maintenance for the trade, Ace and Twitch hit Armory simultaneously.

Callouts: Armory, Weapon Maintenance, Sniper Nest, Garage.

Defense

Essential: Bandit (anti-breach), Mira (intel). Recommended: Wamai (utility denial), Vigil (roam). Flex: Azami (flex / sustain).

Vigil forces attackers to play slow — early picks from off-site keep their utility on flank-watch.

Callouts: Armory, Weapon Maintenance, Sniper Nest, Garage.

Lab / Lab Support (B)

On Lab, Ace and Twitch lead the attack; Kaid and Mira lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Ace (hard breach), Twitch (support). Recommended: Sledge (vertical play), Iana (entry frag). Flex: Finka (flex / intel).

Map control first: Finka and Iana clear Lab Support side while Sledge sets up vertical pressure.

Callouts: Lab, Lab Support, Sniper Nest, Garage.

Defense

Essential: Kaid (anti-breach), Mira (intel). Recommended: Aruni (utility denial), Vigil (roam). Flex: Thunderbird (flex / sustain).

Kaid denies the hard-breach on Lab with electrified walls.

Callouts: Lab, Lab Support, Sniper Nest, Garage.
