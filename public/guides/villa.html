Villa — Complete Strategy Guide

In Rainbow Six Siege, Villa is a ranked map this season with 4 bomb sites: Aviator Room (2F), Trophy Room (2F), Kitchen (1F) and Art Storage (B). Recon 6's plans on Villa lean on Thatcher and Ace for attack and on Smoke and Bandit for defense.

How should you play Villa's bomb sites?

Aviator Room / Games Room (2F)

On Aviator Room, Thermite and Thatcher lead the attack; Bandit and Smoke lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Thermite (hard breach), Thatcher (support). Recommended: Buck (vertical play), Nomad (flank watch). Flex: Iana (intel / entry).

Open the Aviator reinforced wall with Thermite while Thatcher clears electronics.

Callouts: Aviator, Games Room, Study, Astronomy.

Defense

Essential: Bandit (anti-breach), Smoke (area denial). Recommended: Jager (utility denial), Vigil (roam). Flex: Wamai (utility denial).

Bandit tricks the Aviator wall to deny hard breach.

Callouts: Aviator, Games Room, Study, Astronomy.

Trophy Room / Statuary Room (2F)

On Trophy Room, Hibana and Capitao lead the attack; Kaid and Maestro lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Hibana (hard breach), Capitao (area denial). Recommended: Sledge (vertical / soft breach), Zofia (soft breach / entry). Flex: Flores (utility clear).

Hibana opens the Trophy reinforced wall from the balcony side.

Callouts: Trophy, Statuary, Red Stairs, Balcony.

Defense

Essential: Kaid (anti-breach), Maestro (intel / denial). Recommended: Mute (intel denial), Ela (roam). Flex: Melusi (slow / intel).

Kaid electrifies Trophy wall and the hatch above from safe positions.

Callouts: Trophy, Statuary, Red Stairs, Balcony.

Kitchen / Dining Room (1F)

On Kitchen, Ace and Thatcher lead the attack; Mira and Smoke lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Ace (hard breach), Thatcher (support). Recommended: Buck (vertical play), Gridlock (area denial). Flex: Dokkaebi (intel).

Ace opens the Dining Room reinforced wall from mudroom side.

Callouts: Kitchen, Dining, Mudroom, Pantry.

Defense

Essential: Mira (intel / anchor), Smoke (area denial). Recommended: Thunderbird (sustain), Vigil (roam). Flex: Castle (delay).

Mira places a Black Mirror on the wall between Kitchen and pantry for intel on mudroom pushes.

Callouts: Kitchen, Dining, Mudroom, Pantry.

Plan withdrawn in Y11S3. Ubisoft moved this bomb site from the 1F Living Room / Library to Art Storage / Old Office in Villa's reworked basement in Y11S3 (Operation Split Fire).
