Recon 6 — Rainbow Six Siege Map Guides

Recon 6 has a free strategy guide for each of 25 Rainbow Six Siege maps, 14 of them in the current ranked pool. Every guide puts all of a map's bomb sites on one page. Each site lists the operators the attack and defense plans use, a short execute overview and the callouts.

The guides come from the same strat library as the interactive app. Open a guide to learn a map before you queue. Open the app when you want the full plan for one site and side, with the job for each of the five players.

Most guides also list the attacker spawns and room callouts seen in Recon 6's own recorded matches. Those names are read off the in-game labels, and each one appeared on at least two frames. They cover map geography only, not tactics.

Which maps are in the ranked pool?

14 maps with a guide are in the current ranked pool: Bank, Border, Chalet, Clubhouse, Consulate, Fortress, Kafe Dostoyevsky, Kanal, Lair, Nighthaven Labs, Oregon, Theme Park, Villa and Calypso Casino. The list follows Ubisoft's official map index, and Ubisoft rotates the ranked pool at each season launch and midseason. Each card below names a map's bomb sites and the two essential operators each side's plans use most.

  • Bank

    4 bomb sites: CEO Office, Open Area, Tellers' Office and Lockers. Attack leans on Thatcher and Thermite; defense on Bandit and Smoke.

  • Border

    4 bomb sites: Armory Lockers, Workshop, Customs Inspection and Bathroom. Attack leans on Thatcher and Thermite; defense on Smoke and Kaid.

  • Chalet

    4 bomb sites: Master Bedroom, Bar, Kitchen and Wine Cellar. Attack leans on Thatcher and Thermite; defense on Smoke and Bandit.

  • Clubhouse

    4 bomb sites: Cash Room, Bar, Church and Gym. Attack leans on Thatcher and Thermite; defense on Bandit and Smoke.

  • Consulate

    4 bomb sites: Consul Office, Piano Room, Garage and Tellers. Attack leans on Kali and Maverick; defense on Maestro and Bandit.

  • Fortress

    4 bomb sites: Bathroom, Dormitory, Waiting Room and Hammam. Attack leans on Flores and Thermite; defense on Kaid and Valkyrie.

  • Kafe Dostoyevsky

    4 bomb sites: Bar, Reading Room, Mining Room and Kitchen Service. Attack leans on Thatcher and Thermite; defense on Smoke and Kaid.

  • Kanal

    4 bomb sites: Server Room, Security Room, Coast Guard Meeting and Kayaks. Attack leans on Kali and Maverick; defense on Mute and Maestro.

  • Lair

    4 bomb sites: Master Office, Bunks, Armory and Lab. Attack leans on Ace and Twitch; defense on Mira and Mute.

  • Nighthaven Labs

    4 bomb sites: Command, Control, Kitchen and Tank. Attack leans on Ace and Flores; defense on Mira and Mute.

  • Oregon

    4 bomb sites: Kids' Dorms, Meeting Hall, Kitchen and Laundry. Attack leans on Ace and Twitch; defense on Kaid and Mira.

  • Theme Park

    4 bomb sites: Throne Room, Lab, Office and Bunk. Attack leans on Thatcher and Thermite; defense on Smoke and Kaid.

  • Villa

    4 bomb sites: Aviator Room, Trophy Room, Kitchen and Art Storage. Attack leans on Thatcher and Ace; defense on Smoke and Bandit.

  • Calypso Casino

    4 bomb sites: Cigar Room, Blackjack, Bar and CCTV. Attack leans on Thatcher and Thermite; defense on Kaid and Maestro.
