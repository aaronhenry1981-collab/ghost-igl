Oregon — Complete Strategy Guide

In Rainbow Six Siege, Oregon is a ranked map this season with 4 bomb sites: Kids' Dorms (2F), Meeting Hall (1F), Kitchen (1F) and Laundry (B). Recon 6's plans on Oregon lean on Ace and Twitch for attack and on Kaid and Mira for defense.

How should you play Oregon's bomb sites?

Kids' Dorms / Dorms Main Hall (2F)

On Kids' Dorms, Ace and Twitch lead the attack; Kaid and Mira lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Ace (hard breach), Twitch (support). Recommended: Sledge (vertical play), Iana (entry frag). Flex: Finka (flex / intel).

Sledge sets the tempo — Sledge opens floors from above to deny defender anchor positions.

Callouts: Kids Dorms, Dorms Main Hall, Tower, Construction.

Defense

Essential: Kaid (anti-breach), Mira (intel). Recommended: Aruni (utility denial), Ela (roam). Flex: Goyo (flex / sustain).

Setup is intel-first: Mira places a Black Mirror between Kids Dorms and Dorms Main Hall for sightlines on incoming pushes.

Callouts: Kids Dorms, Dorms Main Hall, Tower, Construction.

Meeting Hall / Kitchen (1F)

On Meeting Hall, Thermite and Flores lead the attack; Mute and Valkyrie lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Thermite (hard breach), Flores (support). Recommended: Zofia (vertical play), Zero (entry frag). Flex: Capitao (flex / intel).

Zofia sets the tempo — Zofia plays vertical from 2F to pressure Meeting Hall anchors through the soft floor.

Callouts: Meeting Hall, Kitchen, Tower, Construction.

Defense

Essential: Mute (anti-breach), Valkyrie (intel). Recommended: Jager (utility denial), Pulse (roam). Flex: Azami (flex / sustain).

Setup is intel-first: Valkyrie hides Black Eye cameras outside the site for early rotation and entry reads.

Callouts: Meeting Hall, Kitchen, Tower, Construction.

Kitchen / Dining Hall (1F)

On Kitchen, Hibana and Thatcher lead the attack; Kaid and Echo lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Hibana (hard breach), Thatcher (support). Recommended: Sledge (vertical play), Ash (entry frag). Flex: Lion (flex / intel).

Open with Thatcher clearing electronics, then Hibana hits the Kitchen wall on call.

Callouts: Kitchen, Dining Hall, Tower, Construction.

Defense

Essential: Kaid (anti-breach), Echo (intel). Recommended: Aruni (utility denial), Ela (roam). Flex: Azami (flex / sustain).

Anchored 4, roaming 1: Azami, Aruni, Kaid, and Echo hold site; Ela plays off-site for pick potential and timing pressure.

Callouts: Kitchen, Dining Hall, Tower, Construction.

Laundry / Supply Room (B)

On Laundry, Ace and Twitch lead the attack; Bandit and Mira lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Ace (hard breach), Twitch (support). Recommended: Buck (vertical play), Iana (entry frag). Flex: Nomad (flex / intel).

Buck sets the tempo — Buck opens 1F soft floors above Laundry to deny basement defender setup.

Callouts: Laundry, Supply Room, Tower, Construction.

Defense

Essential: Bandit (anti-breach), Mira (intel). Recommended: Wamai (utility denial), Ela (roam). Flex: Lesion (flex / sustain).

Setup is intel-first: Mira places a Black Mirror between Laundry and Supply Room for sightlines on incoming pushes.

Callouts: Laundry, Supply Room, Tower, Construction.
