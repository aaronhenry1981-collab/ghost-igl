Coastline — Complete Strategy Guide

In Rainbow Six Siege, Coastline is out of the ranked pool this season. Coastline has 4 bomb sites: Hookah Lounge (2F), Theater (2F), Kitchen (1F) and Blue Bar (1F). Recon 6's plans on Coastline lean on Ash and Zofia for attack and on Jager and Smoke for defense.

How should you play Coastline's bomb sites?

Hookah Lounge / Billiards Room (2F)

On Hookah Lounge, Ash and Zofia lead the attack; Jager and Wamai lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Ash (entry frag), Zofia (soft breach). Recommended: Sledge (vertical play), Nomad (flank watch). Flex: Iana (intel).

No hard breach needed — the walls are soft.

Callouts: Hookah, Billiards, VIP, Aqua.

Defense

Essential: Jager (utility denial), Wamai (utility denial). Recommended: Vigil (roam), Valkyrie (intel). Flex: Melusi (slow).

Jager and Wamai stack utility denial — this site gets hit with tons of projectiles.

Callouts: Hookah, Billiards, VIP, Aqua.

Theater / Penthouse (2F)

On Theater, Hibana and Thatcher lead the attack; Kaid and Smoke lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Hibana (hard breach), Thatcher (support). Recommended: Ash (entry frag), Capitao (area denial). Flex: Gridlock (flank denial).

Hibana opens the theater wall from VIP side.

Callouts: Theater, Penthouse, VIP, Hall of Fame.

Defense

Essential: Kaid (anti-breach), Smoke (area denial). Recommended: Jager (utility denial), Ela (roam). Flex: Thunderbird (sustain).

Kaid electrifies the theater wall.

Callouts: Theater, Penthouse, VIP, Hall of Fame.

Kitchen / Service Entrance (1F)

On Kitchen, Zofia and Ash lead the attack; Maestro and Jager lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Zofia (soft breach), Ash (entry frag). Recommended: Buck (vertical play), Nomad (flank watch). Flex: Finka (support).

All soft walls — no hard breach needed.

Callouts: Kitchen, Service, Pool, Sunrise.

Defense

Essential: Maestro (intel / denial), Jager (utility denial). Recommended: Lesion (intel), Vigil (roam). Flex: Wamai (utility denial).

Maestro Evil Eyes cover plant spots and pool entrance.

Callouts: Kitchen, Service, Pool, Sunrise.

Blue Bar / Sunrise Bar (1F)

On Blue Bar, Ash and Zofia lead the attack; Jager and Smoke lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Ash (entry frag), Zofia (soft breach). Recommended: Sledge (vertical play), Nomad (flank watch). Flex: Dokkaebi (intel).

Push from sunrise bar and pool side simultaneously.

Callouts: Blue Bar, Sunrise Bar, Pool, Main Lobby.

Defense

Essential: Jager (utility denial), Smoke (area denial). Recommended: Valkyrie (intel), Ela (roam). Flex: Aruni (utility gate).

Smoke denies plant.

Callouts: Blue Bar, Sunrise Bar, Pool, Main Lobby.
