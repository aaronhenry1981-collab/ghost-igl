House — Complete Strategy Guide

In Rainbow Six Siege, House is out of the ranked pool this season. House has 4 bomb sites: Master Bedroom (2F), Kitchen (1F), Garage (1F) and Boiler (B). Recon 6's plans on House lean on Ace and Twitch for attack and on Mira and Mute for defense.

How should you play House's bomb sites?

Master Bedroom / Car Room (2F)

On Master Bedroom, Thermite and Flores lead the attack; Bandit and Valkyrie lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Thermite (hard breach), Flores (support). Recommended: Buck (vertical play), Zero (entry frag). Flex: Nomad (flex / intel).

Buck sets the tempo — Buck opens floors from above to deny defender anchor positions.

Callouts: Master Bedroom, Closet, Front Yard, Driveway.

Defense

Essential: Bandit (anti-breach), Valkyrie (intel). Recommended: Wamai (utility denial), Vigil (roam). Flex: Thunderbird (flex / sustain).

Setup is intel-first: Valkyrie hides Black Eye cameras outside the site for early rotation and entry reads.

Callouts: Master Bedroom, Closet, Front Yard, Driveway.

Kitchen / Living Room (1F)

On Kitchen, Hibana and Thatcher lead the attack; Kaid and Echo lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Hibana (hard breach), Thatcher (support). Recommended: Sledge (vertical play), Ash (entry frag). Flex: Lion (flex / intel).

Layered execute: Lion flashes from main, Ash pushes Living Room for the trade, Hibana and Thatcher hit Kitchen simultaneously.

Callouts: Kitchen, Living Room, Front Yard, Driveway.

Defense

Essential: Kaid (anti-breach), Echo (intel). Recommended: Aruni (utility denial), Ela (roam). Flex: Lesion (flex / sustain).

Ela forces attackers to play slow — early picks from off-site keep their utility on flank-watch.

Callouts: Kitchen, Living Room, Front Yard, Driveway.
