Nighthaven Labs — Complete Strategy Guide

In Rainbow Six Siege, Nighthaven Labs is a ranked map this season with 4 bomb sites: Command (2F), Control (1F), Kitchen (1F) and Tank (B). Recon 6's plans on Nighthaven Labs lean on Ace and Flores for attack and on Mira and Mute for defense.

How should you play Nighthaven Labs's bomb sites?

Command / Server (2F)

On Command, Thermite and Flores lead the attack; Bandit and Valkyrie lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Thermite (hard breach), Flores (support). Recommended: Buck (vertical play), Zero (entry frag). Flex: Nomad (flex / intel).

Thermite opens the Command reinforced wall while Flores clears anti-breach electronics.

Callouts: Command, Server, Reception, Loading Bay.

Defense

Essential: Bandit (anti-breach), Valkyrie (intel). Recommended: Wamai (utility denial), Caveira (roam). Flex: Azami (flex / sustain).

Hold Server as the trade lane — Azami anchors there.

Callouts: Command, Server, Reception, Loading Bay.

Control / Storage (1F)

On Control, Ace and Twitch lead the attack; Mute and Mira lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Ace (hard breach), Twitch (support). Recommended: Zofia (vertical play), Iana (entry frag). Flex: Capitao (flex / intel).

Map control first: Capitao and Iana clear Storage side while Zofia sets up vertical pressure.

Callouts: Control, Storage, Reception, Loading Bay.

Defense

Essential: Mute (anti-breach), Mira (intel). Recommended: Jager (utility denial), Caveira (roam). Flex: Goyo (flex / sustain).

Mute denies the hard-breach on Control with electrified walls.

Callouts: Control, Storage, Reception, Loading Bay.

Kitchen / Cafeteria (1F)

On Kitchen, Ace and Twitch lead the attack; Kaid and Mira lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Ace (hard breach), Twitch (support). Recommended: Sledge (vertical play), Iana (entry frag). Flex: Finka (flex / intel).

Map control first: Finka and Iana clear Cafeteria side while Sledge sets up vertical pressure.

Callouts: Kitchen, Cafeteria, Reception, Loading Bay.

Defense

Essential: Kaid (anti-breach), Mira (intel). Recommended: Aruni (utility denial), Caveira (roam). Flex: Lesion (flex / sustain).

Kaid denies the hard-breach on Kitchen with electrified walls.

Callouts: Kitchen, Cafeteria, Reception, Loading Bay.

Tank / Assembly (B)

On Tank, Thermite and Flores lead the attack; Mute and Valkyrie lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Thermite (hard breach), Flores (support). Recommended: Zofia (vertical play), Zero (entry frag). Flex: Capitao (flex / intel).

Zofia sets the tempo — Zofia opens 1F soft floors above Tank to deny basement defender setup.

Callouts: Tank, Assembly, Reception, Loading Bay.

Defense

Essential: Mute (anti-breach), Valkyrie (intel). Recommended: Jager (utility denial), Vigil (roam). Flex: Lesion (flex / sustain).

Setup is intel-first: Valkyrie hides Black Eye cameras outside the site for early rotation and entry reads.

Callouts: Tank, Assembly, Reception, Loading Bay.
