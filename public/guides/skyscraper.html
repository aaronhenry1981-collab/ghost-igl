Skyscraper — Complete Strategy Guide

In Rainbow Six Siege, Skyscraper is out of the ranked pool this season. Skyscraper has 4 bomb sites: Tea Room (2F), Work Office (2F), Kitchen (1F) and Bedroom (1F). Recon 6's plans on Skyscraper lean on Thatcher and Ace for attack and on Smoke and Bandit for defense.

How should you play Skyscraper's bomb sites?

Tea Room / Karaoke (2F)

On Tea Room, Buck and Ash lead the attack; Smoke and Mute lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Buck (vertical play), Ash (entry frag). Recommended: Nomad (flank watch), Zofia (soft breach). Flex: Iana (intel / entry).

Buck plays vertical from the roof hatch area to destroy the Tea Room floor and force defenders off anchors.

Callouts: Tea Room, Karaoke, Geisha, Dragon.

Defense

Essential: Smoke (area denial), Mute (intel denial). Recommended: Vigil (roam), Jager (utility denial). Flex: Thunderbird (sustain).

Smoke holds Tea Room doorways and denies plant attempts with canisters in the last 30 seconds.

Callouts: Tea Room, Karaoke, Geisha, Dragon.

Work Office / Exhibition (2F)

On Work Office, Ace and Twitch lead the attack; Mira and Smoke lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Ace (hard breach), Twitch (support). Recommended: Sledge (vertical / soft breach), Capitao (area denial). Flex: Lion (intel).

Ace opens the Work Office reinforced wall from the exterior.

Callouts: Work Office, Exhibition, Hallway, Main Stairs.

Defense

Essential: Mira (intel / anchor), Smoke (area denial). Recommended: Mozzie (intel denial), Pulse (intel). Flex: Castle (delay).

Mira window between Exhibition and Work Office gives intel on the push.

Callouts: Work Office, Exhibition, Hallway, Main Stairs.

Kitchen / BBQ (1F)

On Kitchen, Thermite and Thatcher lead the attack; Bandit and Smoke lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Thermite (hard breach), Thatcher (support). Recommended: Buck (vertical play), Nomad (flank watch). Flex: Flores (utility clear).

Thermite opens the Kitchen reinforced wall from the exterior while Thatcher clears electronics.

Callouts: Kitchen, BBQ, Pantry, Dragon.

Defense

Essential: Bandit (anti-breach), Smoke (area denial). Recommended: Maestro (intel / denial), Vigil (roam). Flex: Wamai (utility denial).

Bandit tricks the Kitchen wall to deny Thermite.

Callouts: Kitchen, BBQ, Pantry, Dragon.

Bedroom / Bathroom (1F)

On Bedroom, Hibana and Thatcher lead the attack; Kaid and Lesion lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Hibana (hard breach), Thatcher (support). Recommended: Sledge (vertical play), Gridlock (area denial). Flex: Dokkaebi (intel).

Hibana opens the reinforced Bedroom wall from balcony while Thatcher clears electronics.

Callouts: Bedroom, Closet, Bathroom, Balcony.

Defense

Essential: Kaid (anti-breach), Lesion (intel / delay). Recommended: Jager (utility denial), Ela (roam). Flex: Aruni (utility gate).

Kaid electrifies the Bedroom wall to deny hard breach.

Callouts: Bedroom, Closet, Bathroom, Balcony.
