Outback — Complete Strategy Guide

In Rainbow Six Siege, Outback is out of the ranked pool this season. Outback has 4 bomb sites: Laundry (2F), Party (2F), Green Bedroom (1F) and Mechanic Shop (1F). Recon 6's plans on Outback lean on Kali and Maverick for attack and on Maestro and Mute for defense.

How should you play Outback's bomb sites?

Laundry / Piano (2F)

On Laundry, Maverick and Kali lead the attack; Bandit and Maestro lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Maverick (hard breach), Kali (support). Recommended: Buck (vertical play), Ying (entry frag). Flex: Glaz (flex / intel).

Open with Kali clearing electronics, then Maverick hits the Laundry wall on call.

Callouts: Laundry, Piano Room, Reception, Bushranger.

Defense

Essential: Bandit (anti-breach), Maestro (intel). Recommended: Wamai (utility denial), Alibi (roam). Flex: Mozzie (flex / sustain).

Anchored 4, roaming 1: Mozzie, Wamai, Bandit, and Maestro hold site; Alibi plays off-site for pick potential and timing pressure.

Callouts: Laundry, Piano Room, Reception, Bushranger.

Party / Office (2F)

On Party, Maverick and Kali lead the attack; Mute and Maestro lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Maverick (hard breach), Kali (support). Recommended: Zofia (vertical play), Ying (entry frag). Flex: Fuze (flex / intel).

Maverick opens the Party Room reinforced wall while Kali clears anti-breach electronics.

Callouts: Party Room, Office, Reception, Bushranger.

Defense

Essential: Mute (anti-breach), Maestro (intel). Recommended: Jager (utility denial), Caveira (roam). Flex: Doc (flex / sustain).

Hold Office as the trade lane — Doc anchors there.

Callouts: Party Room, Office, Reception, Bushranger.

Green Bedroom / Red Bedroom (1F)

On Green Bedroom, Maverick and Kali lead the attack; Mute and Maestro lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Maverick (hard breach), Kali (support). Recommended: Zofia (vertical play), Ying (entry frag). Flex: Fuze (flex / intel).

Layered execute: Fuze flashes from main, Ying pushes Red Bedroom for the trade, Maverick and Kali hit Green Bedroom simultaneously.

Callouts: Green Bedroom, Red Bedroom, Reception, Bushranger.

Defense

Essential: Mute (anti-breach), Maestro (intel). Recommended: Jager (utility denial), Alibi (roam). Flex: Lesion (flex / sustain).

Alibi forces attackers to play slow — early picks from off-site keep their utility on flank-watch.

Callouts: Green Bedroom, Red Bedroom, Reception, Bushranger.
