Emerald Plains — Complete Strategy Guide

In Rainbow Six Siege, Emerald Plains is out of the ranked pool this season. Emerald Plains has 4 bomb sites: Administration (2F), Private Gallery (2F), Bar (1F) and Kitchen (1F). Recon 6's plans on Emerald Plains lean on Hibana and Thatcher for attack and on Echo and Mute for defense.

How should you play Emerald Plains's bomb sites?

Administration / CEO Office (2F)

On Administration, Ace and Twitch lead the attack; Kaid and Mira lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Ace (hard breach), Twitch (support). Recommended: Sledge (vertical play), Iana (entry frag). Flex: Finka (flex / intel).

Layered execute: Finka flashes from main, Iana pushes CEO Office for the trade, Ace and Twitch hit Administration simultaneously.

Callouts: Administration, CEO Office, Foyer, Main Stairs.

Defense

Essential: Kaid (anti-breach), Mira (intel). Recommended: Aruni (utility denial), Pulse (roam). Flex: Thunderbird (flex / sustain).

Pulse forces attackers to play slow — early picks from off-site keep their utility on flank-watch.

Callouts: Administration, CEO Office, Foyer, Main Stairs.

Bar / Lounge (1F)

On Bar, Hibana and Thatcher lead the attack; Bandit and Echo lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Hibana (hard breach), Thatcher (support). Recommended: Buck (vertical play), Ash (entry frag). Flex: Glaz (flex / intel).

Open with Thatcher clearing electronics, then Hibana hits the Bar wall on call.

Callouts: Bar, Lounge, Foyer, Main Stairs.

Defense

Essential: Bandit (anti-breach), Echo (intel). Recommended: Wamai (utility denial), Vigil (roam). Flex: Thunderbird (flex / sustain).

Anchored 4, roaming 1: Thunderbird, Wamai, Bandit, and Echo hold site; Vigil plays off-site for pick potential and timing pressure.

Callouts: Bar, Lounge, Foyer, Main Stairs.
