Clubhouse — Complete Strategy Guide

In Rainbow Six Siege, Clubhouse is a ranked map this season with 4 bomb sites: Cash Room (2F), Bar (1F), Church (B) and Gym (2F). Recon 6's plans on Clubhouse lean on Thatcher and Thermite for attack and on Bandit and Smoke for defense.

How should you play Clubhouse's bomb sites?

Cash Room / CCTV Room (2F)

On Cash Room, Thermite and Maverick lead the attack; Bandit and Smoke lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Thermite (hard breach), Maverick (support breach). Recommended: Sledge (vertical play), Nomad (flank watch). Flex: Iana (intel).

Thermite opens the cash room wall from construction.

Callouts: Cash Room, CCTV, Construction, Master Bedroom.

Defense

Essential: Bandit (anti-breach), Smoke (area denial). Recommended: Mute (intel denial), Vigil (roam). Flex: Wamai (utility denial).

Bandit trick the cash wall to deny Thermite.

Callouts: Cash Room, CCTV, Construction, Master Bedroom.
