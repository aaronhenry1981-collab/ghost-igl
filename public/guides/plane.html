Presidential Plane — Complete Strategy Guide

In Rainbow Six Siege, Presidential Plane is out of the ranked pool this season. Presidential Plane has 4 bomb sites: Meeting Room (2F), Security Room (1F), Kitchen (1F) and Work Room (1F). Recon 6's plans on Presidential Plane lean on Flores and Hibana for attack and on Echo and Mute for defense.

How should you play Presidential Plane's bomb sites?

Meeting Room / Office (2F)

On Meeting Room, Hibana and Thatcher lead the attack; Kaid and Echo lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Hibana (hard breach), Thatcher (support). Recommended: Sledge (vertical play), Ash (entry frag). Flex: Lion (flex / intel).

Layered execute: Lion flashes from main, Ash pushes Office for the trade, Hibana and Thatcher hit Meeting Room simultaneously.

Callouts: Meeting Room, Office, Tarmac, Front Stairs.

Defense

Essential: Kaid (anti-breach), Echo (intel). Recommended: Aruni (utility denial), Ela (roam). Flex: Lesion (flex / sustain).

Ela forces attackers to play slow — early picks from off-site keep their utility on flank-watch.

Callouts: Meeting Room, Office, Tarmac, Front Stairs.

Security Room / Cargo Hold (1F)

On Security Room, Thermite and Flores lead the attack; Mute and Valkyrie lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Thermite (hard breach), Flores (support). Recommended: Zofia (vertical play), Zero (entry frag). Flex: Capitao (flex / intel).

Open with Flores clearing electronics, then Thermite hits the Security Room wall on call.

Callouts: Security Room, Cargo Hold, Tarmac, Front Stairs.

Defense

Essential: Mute (anti-breach), Valkyrie (intel). Recommended: Jager (utility denial), Vigil (roam). Flex: Thunderbird (flex / sustain).

Anchored 4, roaming 1: Thunderbird, Jager, Mute, and Valkyrie hold site; Vigil plays off-site for pick potential and timing pressure.

Callouts: Security Room, Cargo Hold, Tarmac, Front Stairs.

Kitchen / Stateroom (1F)

On Kitchen, Thermite and Flores lead the attack; Bandit and Valkyrie lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Thermite (hard breach), Flores (support). Recommended: Buck (vertical play), Zero (entry frag). Flex: Nomad (flex / intel).

Map control first: Nomad and Zero clear Stateroom side while Buck sets up vertical pressure.

Callouts: Kitchen, Stateroom, Tarmac, Front Stairs.

Defense

Essential: Bandit (anti-breach), Valkyrie (intel). Recommended: Wamai (utility denial), Alibi (roam). Flex: Thunderbird (flex / sustain).

Bandit denies the hard-breach on Kitchen with electrified walls.

Callouts: Kitchen, Stateroom, Tarmac, Front Stairs.

Work Room / Lobby (1F)

On Work Room, Hibana and Thatcher lead the attack; Mute and Echo lead the defense.

Attack

Essential: Hibana (hard breach), Thatcher (support). Recommended: Zofia (vertical play), Ash (entry frag). Flex: Fuze (flex / intel).

Map control first: Fuze and Ash clear Lobby side while Zofia sets up vertical pressure.

Callouts: Work Room, Lobby, Tarmac, Front Stairs.

Defense

Essential: Mute (anti-breach), Echo (intel). Recommended: Jager (utility denial), Caveira (roam). Flex: Lesion (flex / sustain).

Mute denies the hard-breach on Work Room with electrified walls.

Callouts: Work Room, Lobby, Tarmac, Front Stairs.
