## Deploying next time

- **Guards.** `deploy.ps1` refuses:
  - a dirty tree;
  - any HEAD other than `origin/content/strat-beta-disclaimer`;
  - a build without `VITE_CUSTOMER_SUCCESS_API_URL`, `VITE_PLAYER_DATA_API_URL` and `VITE_SUPPORT_UI` (see `.env.example`).
- **Upload order.** Assets first, then pages, with no `--delete`.
- **Canonical release directory.** `C:\IronFront_Master\ghost-igl-production`: a clean checkout of `content/strat-beta-disclaimer` with `.env.production` set. Update it with `git pull --ff-only`. The old `C:\IronFront_Master\ghost-igl` checkout (at `5ae6113`, its uncommitted work captured here as `5d57a22`) is retired for deploys: all five of its deploy scripts stop with "REFUSING TO DEPLOY".
