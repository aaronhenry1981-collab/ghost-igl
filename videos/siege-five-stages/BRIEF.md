## Intent

Third short-form video for TikTok / YouTube Shorts / Reels, after
`siege-teammate-quotes` (roast the teammate) and `siege-lost-the-round`
(roast the viewer). This one deliberately breaks the countdown-list pattern
those two share, so the channel doesn't read as one template on repeat.

Format is the five-stages-of-grief parody mapped onto a losing ranked match:
denial, anger, bargaining, depression, acceptance. The difference from a list
is the arc — these land in a fixed emotional order everyone recognises, so the
payoff is cumulative rather than five parallel jokes.

The brand tag is the punchline this time rather than a tag bolted on the end:
"There's a sixth stage. Fixing it." Acceptance sets it up, so the ad *is* the
joke's resolution.

Tone is dry and deadpan. No hype, no corporate voice, no emoji.

## Customizations

- Registry component `rgb-glitch-text` drives the hook card's chromatic tear.
- The counter slot carries stage names rather than `01 / 05`, so it's set a
  little smaller with tighter tracking than the sibling projects — "BARGAINING"
  needs the room.
- Vertical 1080x1920 with generous platform-UI safe margins (top 15%, bottom 20%).
- Silent by design — built to be read on mute.
