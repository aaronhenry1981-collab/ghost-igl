# Posting pack

Upload-and-paste copy for the videos in this directory. Each renders to
1080x1920 and is built to be read on mute, so the on-screen text carries the
joke and the caption only has to start an argument in the comments.

Three rules that apply to every post:

- **No rank, stat, win-rate or result claims.** Not in the video, not in the
  caption, not in a reply. Same rule as the site.
- **No founding-rate urgency.** That window closed 2026-08-31. Any "locked in
  for life, ends soon" copy is now a false promise — do not reuse the old
  launch lines.
- **Link in bio, not in the caption.** Shorts and TikTok both bury captions
  with outbound links. The video already ends on `r6coaching.com`.

---

## 1. `siege-teammate-quotes` — "Things your teammate says right before they throw the round"

The teammate roast. Safest opener of the three because the viewer is never the
target — they're in on it.

**Title (Shorts / Reels):** Things your teammate says right before they throw the round

**Caption:** Which one did you hear tonight. Be specific.

**Alt captions:**
- "Trust." is the most dangerous word in this game.
- I got roam = I'll see you next round

**Hashtags:** `#rainbowsixsiege #r6 #r6s #siege #ranked #gaming #fps`

**Comment hook:** Pin *"Add the one I missed."* — the list format invites
people to supply number six, which is the cheapest engagement this format has.

---

## 2. `siege-lost-the-round` — "Five reasons you lost that round"

The self-roast. Flips the target from the teammate to the viewer, which reads
harsher — post it *after* video 1 has been up a while, so the audience already
knows the voice and takes it as a bit rather than a lecture.

**Title:** Five reasons you lost that round

**Caption:** Number 5 is personal and you know it.

**Alt captions:**
- No drone, just vibes.
- It was never your aim.

**Hashtags:** `#rainbowsixsiege #r6 #r6s #siege #ranked #gaming #fps`

**Comment hook:** Expect "it's not me it's my team" replies. Don't argue — reply
*"that's video one"* and link the teammate one. The two videos feed each other.

---

## 3. `siege-five-stages` — "The five stages of losing in ranked"

The grief-parody. Different shape from the two list videos, so use it to break
up the run rather than posting all three back to back.

**Title:** The five stages of losing in ranked

**Caption:** Which stage are you on right now.

**Alt captions:**
- "Who banned Thatcher?" — you did.
- There's a sixth stage.

**Hashtags:** `#rainbowsixsiege #r6 #r6s #siege #ranked #gaming #fps`

**Comment hook:** *"Who banned Thatcher"* is the beat that needs R6 literacy.
It will pull "real ones know" replies — that's the audience worth having.

---

## Sequencing

Order matters more than cadence here: teammate roast first to establish the
voice, then the grief parody, then the self-roast once people trust the tone.
Space them out rather than dumping all three — three posts in a day competes
with itself for the same viewers.

## What I can't tell you

I have no analytics access from this repo, so nothing above is a performance
prediction — it's copy and a running order, not data. Once these have been up
long enough to compare, the retention curve on the hook card (first 2.6s) is
the number worth reading: if viewers drop before the first beat lands at 2.6s,
the hook copy is the problem, not the joke.
